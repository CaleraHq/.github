# CALERA

**Simple scheduling, beautifully made.**

Calera is a modern scheduling platform for individuals and teams to share availability, manage bookings, and embed customizable scheduling experiences anywhere.

## ✦ Features

- 📅 Calendar and availability management
- 🔗 Public booking pages and links
- 🧩 Customizable embeddable widgets
- 🔔 Email and push notifications
- 🎥 Google Meet and video integrations
- 👥 Bookings, contacts, and team management
- 🎨 Light and dark mode
- ⚡ Fast, minimal scheduling experience

## 📦 Project

Calera is built with a focus on simplicity, flexibility, and a polished scheduling experience.

> Schedule less. Get more done.
